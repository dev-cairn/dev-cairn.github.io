The Cairn Manifesto

A Sovereign Desktop Browser.
Built to Protect, Not to Harvest.

Modern commercial browsers have evolved into surveillance engines designed to track your reading habits, log your searches, and tether your identity to remote cloud infrastructure. Cairn is engineered as an unapologetic countermeasure: a pure, sovereign desktop workspace that belongs exclusively to you.

Sovereign Local Vault

Your passwords, session data, notes, and pinned tabs are secured directly on your machine with military-grade AES-256 encryption. No sync servers, no remote tracking databases, and zero third-party telemetry. You hold the master key.

Dual-Engine Split View

Research without window clutter. Cairn’s native side-by-side split screen allows you to run two live web pages, documentation, or video streams simultaneously on a single display with seamless ratio adjustments and independent browsing contexts.

Multi-Hop Onion Circuit

Route your traffic through decentralized multi-hop SOCKS5 privacy circuits with automatic DNS leak shielding and WebRTC suppression. Browse native .onion hidden services with complete network anonymity.

Serverless P2P Meetings

Conduct instant end-to-end encrypted video and voice conferences directly browser-to-browser. No logins, no room reservations, and no corporate intermediary recording your confidential discussions.

Ephemeral Masked Mode

When privacy demands zero trace, Masked Mode spins up an ephemeral, memory-only session. Cookies, cache, DOM storage, and navigation trails are instantly vaporized the instant the tab or window closes.

Hardware PIN Lock

Step away from your workstation with peace of mind. Cairn features a cryptographic lock screen secured with PBKDF2 key derivation (100,000 iterations). Instantly secure your open work with Ctrl+Shift+L.

Step 0 • Required Tool

Download SOCKS5 Privacy Client (Tor)

To route web requests through the Onion network, Cairn pairs with an active Tor SOCKS5 daemon running on your local machine. For maximum security and separation of privilege, we recommend running the official Tor Browser in the background.

Recommended for Everyone

Tor Browser Bundle

The simplest setup. Install and launch Tor Browser, minimize it to your taskbar, and it automatically exposes an authenticated SOCKS5 proxy on 127.0.0.1:9150.

For Advanced / Developers

Tor Expert Daemon

Headless background Windows service. Runs silently without a UI window on default port 127.0.0.1:9050.

winget install TorProject.Tor
1

Start Your Tor Service

Launch Tor Browser (or your background Tor service). Connect to the Tor network. Once connected, minimize the window. Keep it running in the background so Cairn can establish its encrypted SOCKS5 pipeline.

💡 Port Reference: 127.0.0.1:9150 (Tor Browser) or 127.0.0.1:9050 (Tor Daemon).
2

Activate Onion Mode in Cairn

In Cairn, click the Shield Icon in the address bar (or navigate to Settings → Onion Routing Mode). Select your port (9150 or 9050) and toggle "Enable Onion Routing Mode" on.

✓ Cairn will instantly re-bind its proxy socket, activate remote DNS resolution, and suppress WebRTC.
3

Verify Your Circuit & Browse Anonymously

Click "Verify Privacy Connection" in Cairn Settings or open https://check.torproject.org. Cairn will confirm your encrypted exit node IP and latency. You can now freely browse both regular sites and native .onion hidden addresses.

🧅 Click "New Identity" anytime in Cairn to wipe session cookies and rebuild your circuit path.
⛔

Do Not Login to KYC Accounts

Never sign into your real-name banking, email, or social accounts while in Onion Mode. Doing so associates your identity with the exit node.

🚨

Zero Local History Recorded

All websites visited during Onion Mode are quarantined from your history, recent tabs, and search cache automatically.

🛡️

Built-in LAN Firewall

Cairn automatically blocks local and private RFC-1918 subnets while Onion Mode is running to prevent internal intranet probes.
